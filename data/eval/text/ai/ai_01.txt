Certainly! Here's a simple weeknight garlic butter shrimp recipe:

Ingredients: - 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined - 4 tbsp butter - 4 cloves garlic, minced - Juice of half a lemon - Salt, pepper, and chopped parsley

Instructions: 1. Pat the shrimp dry and season with salt and pepper. 2. Melt the butter over medium heat, then sauté the garlic for about 30 seconds. 3. Add the shrimp and cook 2 minutes per side, until pink and opaque. 4. Finish with lemon juice and parsley, toss, and serve immediately.

This pairs beautifully with rice or crusty bread. Let me know if you'd like a dairy-free or spicier variation!
