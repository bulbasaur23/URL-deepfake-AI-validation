Here's a relaxed 3-day Lisbon itinerary:

Day 1 — Historic center: start in Baixa, ride Tram 28 to Alfama, get lost in its alleys, then visit São Jorge Castle before sunset. Dinner: a tascas-style spot with grilled sardines.

Day 2 — Belém: pastéis de nata at the original bakery, then the Jerónimos Monastery, the Tower of Belém, and the riverside walk. Afternoon: the MAAT museum.

Day 3 — Views and neighborhoods: Miradouro da Senhora do Monte in the morning, lunch in Príncipe Real, then the LX Factory for shops and street art. Finish with fado in Bairro Alto.

Pace is deliberately light — two anchors per day, not five. Want me to swap a day for a Sintra side trip?
