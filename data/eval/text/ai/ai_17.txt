Before you file, make sure you have these documents ready:

- Last year's tax return (for carryovers and comparison) - All W-2s and 1099s (including interest and dividend forms) - Records of estimated tax payments you made during the year - Receipts for deductible expenses: charitable donations, medical costs above the threshold, and eligible education expenses - Your bank routing details for direct deposit or payment

Also check: did you change jobs, move states, or get married? Any of those changes your withholding and credits. I'm not a tax professional and this isn't tax advice — for your specific situation, confirm with a licensed preparer.
