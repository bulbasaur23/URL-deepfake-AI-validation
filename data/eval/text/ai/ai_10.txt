When choosing a blender, weigh these four factors:

1. Motor power — 700–1000 W is fine for smoothies; 1200 W+ handles ice and nut butters. 2. Blade and jar design — angled stainless blades plus a tapered jar reduce pockets that stop the vortex. 3. Noise — personal blenders are louder per watt; look for a sound shield if you blend early in the morning. 4. Cleaning — dishwasher-safe, wide-base jars save the most time.
