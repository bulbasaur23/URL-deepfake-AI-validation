The Lantern Who Was Afraid of the Dark

In a small harbor town lived a lantern named Pip. Every evening the other lamps glowed proudly, but Pip trembled, because inside him the flame was small.

One night a fog rolled in, thicker than wool, and the big lamps flickered out. A lost kitten mewed at the end of the pier. Pip took a deep breath and let his little flame stand up as tall as it could — and it was just enough. Step by step, the kitten followed the light home.

The harbor master said, "Pip, you were the brightest lamp tonight." Pip learned that courage isn't a big flame; it's a small one that refuses to go out.
