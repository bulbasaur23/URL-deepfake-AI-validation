You can accomplish this with a single JOIN:

SELECT o.id, o.total, c.name FROM orders o JOIN customers c ON c.id = o.customer_id WHERE o.created_at >= DATE '2026-01-01' AND o.status = 'paid' ORDER BY o.created_at DESC;

Two notes: make sure there's an index on `orders(customer_id, created_at)` — the planner will use it to avoid a scan — and if a customer can have no orders yet but you still want them listed, switch to a LEFT JOIN from customers to orders.

Happy to adapt this to your exact schema if you paste the table definitions.
