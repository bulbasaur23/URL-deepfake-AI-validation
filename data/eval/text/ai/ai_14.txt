Here's a haiku about the first snowfall, plus a quick explanation:

First snow holds the street — every parked car wears a soft white hood, hushed and still.

The poem follows the 5-7-5 syllable pattern and leans on a single image: cars "wearing" snow like hoods, which turns the ordinary street into something patient and quiet. The dash after the first line creates the pause that mirrors the hush.
