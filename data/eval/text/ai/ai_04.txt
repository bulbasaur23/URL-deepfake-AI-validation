Great question! In simple terms, photosynthesis is how plants make their own food using sunlight. There are two main parts:

1. Light-dependent reactions — the plant captures sunlight with chlorophyll and uses it to split water molecules, releasing oxygen as a byproduct. 2. The Calvin cycle — the plant takes in carbon dioxide from the air and uses the energy captured earlier to build glucose, a sugar it stores for energy.

The overall equation is: carbon dioxide + water + light → glucose + oxygen.

In short: plants breathe in CO2, drink water, catch sunlight, and produce sugar and the oxygen we breathe. Let me know if you'd like a diagram-friendly version!
